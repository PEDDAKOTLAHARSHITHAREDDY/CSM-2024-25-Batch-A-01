# CSM-2024-25-Batch-A-01
CSM 2024 – 25 Batch: A – 01
                              ABSTRACT 
                              
Early detection of potato leaf diseases is vital for crop health and yield. 
Traditional manual inspection is time-consuming, labor-intensive, and prone to errors. 
To overcome these issues, this study presents an automated deep learning-based 
system to detect and classify potato leaf diseases: Early Blight, Late Blight, and 
Healthy. The goal is to offer a fast, accurate, and accessible solution to help farmers 
make timely decisions. 

In this research, we utilize a MobileNet-based Convolutional Neural 
Network (CNN) for disease classification. MobileNet is chosen for its lightweight and 
efficient architecture, making it ideal for deployment in resource-constrained 
environments such as mobile devices and embedded systems. The model is trained on 
a comprehensive dataset of potato leaf images, enabling it to extract and analyze 
critical features such as color, texture, and shape for accurate disease identification. 
Additionally, the model provides a confidence score for each prediction, reflecting the 
certainty of the classification, and offers organic and chemical treatment 
recommendations for the detected disease. 

The results of this study demonstrate the robustness of the MobileNet
based CNN in accurately classifying potato leaf diseases, with minimal 
misclassifications. By automating the disease detection process, this research 
contributes to the advancement of precision agriculture, enabling early intervention to 
prevent crop loss and improve yield. The integration of treatment recommendations 
further enhances the system's usability, making it a comprehensive solution for 
modern farming practices. 

Keywords: Potato leaf disease detection, deep learning, Convolutional Neural 
Networks (CNN), MobileNet, Early Blight, Late Blight, precision agriculture, 
confidence score, treatment recommendations.
